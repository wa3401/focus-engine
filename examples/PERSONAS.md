# Example personas

Three fake people, used for the example stacks in this folder, the demo in the talk, and as the base for many eval fixtures. All names, companies and domains are invented.

## Maya Chen — VP of Sales (full engine, demo persona)

- **Company:** Northwind Analytics (`northwind.example`), 40-person B2B SaaS.
- **Tool:** Claude Max, personal-ish work account, no admin blocks. Connectors: Slack, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, HubSpot. Scheduled tasks and sub-agents available.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8:00–17:30, America/New_York. Quiet hours after 19:00 and weekends.
- **Priorities:** 1) Close Q4: Acme renewal ($120k) and Globex expansion by Dec 15. 2) Hire two account executives by end of January. 3) Launch the new partner program in Q1.
- **People:** Dana Whitfield (CEO, VIP), Sam Ortiz and Lena Park (AEs, reports), Raj Mehta (RevOps), Priya Shah (Acme champion, VIP external), Tom Becker (Globex buyer), Jordan Lee (recruiter, waiting-on).
- **Noise:** vendor newsletters, #random, #social, automated HubSpot digests.
- **Helpers:** all four. Brief 7:30 weekdays to Slack DM (thread replies + reactions). Meeting prep folded into the brief for external meetings + Dana, plus a card 15 min before external calls. Shutdown 17:00 interactive in Slack. Audit every other Friday in chat.
