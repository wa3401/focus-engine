## Feedback in one line

Type it in the **Pending updates** box at the bottom of your Profile, and paste each delivery's "Profile updates" block there too.
Example: `done 2, snooze 3 to Mon, mute the Nonprofit Pulse newsletter, Marcus is a VIP`
The next brief starts with "Applied: …" so you can see it landed.

**Friday tidy:** open the Profile in Word, ask Copilot "apply Pending updates to the data block and clear the box," and check the result.

## Change things

Open a chat and say it: "move my brief to 8:00," "add the Finance channel," "turn on meeting outcomes in the wrap-up." You'll get revised text to paste over the scheduled prompt (Copilot Chat › … › Scheduled prompts › Edit).

## Pause, upgrade, remove

- **Pause:** turn off both scheduled prompts in the Scheduled prompts list and set "Vacation mode: on until <date>" in your Profile. Turn them back on when you return; the first brief catches you up.
- **Upgrade:** "upgrade my focus engine" with a newer setup prompt attached. You'll see a few bullets per helper, then new text to paste.
- **Check by Feb 17, 2027:** open Scheduled prompts and confirm both still show as active.
- **Remove:**
