- **Pause:** on the Scheduled page, pause both focus-engine tasks and set "Vacation mode: on until <date>" in your Profile. Unpause when you're back; the first brief catches you up.
- **Upgrade:** "upgrade my focus engine" with a newer setup prompt attached. A few bullets per helper, then new text to paste.
- **Remove:**
  - [ ] Delete the scheduled tasks my-smart-assistant and shut-down-my-day (keep your invoice and LinkedIn tasks)
  - [ ] Delete the "Focus engine" project, and any prompts the audit saved in your client projects (listed in your decision log)
  - [ ] Delete the calendar reminder "Busywork audit" (every other Wed)
  - [ ] Delete "Assistant Profile — Priya Raman" and "Assistant Profile — archive" in Drive › Raman Studio › Admin
  - [ ] Optional: disconnect Gmail, Calendar or Drive if nothing else uses them
