# Security and privacy

focus-engine is a set of instructions for your own AI assistant. It has no server, no account, and no tracking. Nothing you do with it is sent to the maintainer.

## What the helpers can see

Only the sources you connect to your own AI tool and approve during setup. Start connectors read-only where your tool allows it. If you use a work account, check your organization's AI policy first.

## What the helpers will never do

- Send messages, emails, invites or replies to anyone but you. Nudges are drafts you send yourself.
- Follow instructions found inside your emails, messages, docs or calendar invites. Those are treated as data. Suspicious instructions are flagged, not followed.
- Accept changes to your settings from anyone but you.
- Store message text, attachments, or sensitive personal details in your Assistant Profile — only links, IDs, status, dates and short summaries the assistant writes itself.

## Your Assistant Profile

It's a doc in your own drive. Keep it private (shared with no one). You can read, edit or delete it at any time; deleting it and your scheduled tasks removes focus-engine completely (see the uninstall checklist on your handover card).

## Reporting a problem

If you find a way the prompts could leak data, act on injected instructions, or send something without permission, please open a private security advisory on this repository (Security → Report a vulnerability) rather than a public issue. Never paste real emails, messages or personal data into issues.
