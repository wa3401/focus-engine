# Interview guide (memory first)

## Before asking anything

Look through what you already have: saved memory, custom instructions, recent past chats (where you can search them), connected tools (calendar patterns, frequent email/Slack contacts, CRM ownership). Draft answers for everything below. Then show a short pre-fill and ask them to confirm or correct:

> "Here's what I already know — fix anything that's off:
> • You're a product lead at Acme, working on the Q1 launch and hiring two engineers
> • You work 8:30–5:30 Eastern, Mon–Fri
> • People you deal with most: Dana (your manager), Sam and Priya (reports), Acme's design agency
> Anything wrong or missing?"

## Ask only what's missing (one or two at a time)

1. **Role, in one line.** "How would you describe your job to a new colleague?"
2. **This quarter.** "What does a great next three months look like?" → turn into 3–5 ranked priorities with a one-line why each. Confirm the ranking.
3. **People.** "Whose messages should always reach you? Who are you usually waiting on?" → VIPs, reports, clients, waiting-ons.
4. **Noise.** "What do you never want to see in a brief?" (newsletters, certain channels, automated alerts, FYI from certain tools).
5. **Hours.** Working days and hours, time zone (IANA, e.g. America/New_York), quiet hours, travel patterns.
6. **Where you want things.** Email, Slack/Teams DM, in the AI app, a doc. Phone or desktop first.
7. **What good looks like.** "After a week, how would you know this is working?"

## Style

- Offer a default with every question ("most people pick 7:30am — want that?").
- Accept "skip" and "default"; never push.
- Keep a running summary they can see; confirm it before generating.
- Don't collect sensitive personal details. If they volunteer them, don't store them in the Profile.
